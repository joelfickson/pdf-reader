# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, keep feedback about the work, and do not harass anyone.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through the contact details on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/joelfickson). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly or seriously break it.
