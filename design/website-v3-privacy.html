Privacy

Your documents stay yours.

Agentic PDF reads your files on your own computer. This page explains, in plain language, what the app does with your data, what it keeps, and how to remove it.

Last updated 7 November 2026
No account.

There is nothing to sign up for or sign in to. You download the app and open a file.

No tracking.

The reader's code contains no telemetry, no analytics and no network requests.

No uploads.

Documents are opened and rendered on your computer by the PDFium engine. They are never sent anywhere.

The reader

What happens when you open a PDF

The app reads the file from your disk, renders its pages and extracts its text on your computer. Page images and text are kept in memory while you read and are released when you close the tab.

When you copy text, the selected text is placed on your system clipboard, as with any app. Nothing else leaves the reader.

Version 0.1.2 writes no files of its own to your computer.

Stored on your computer

A short list of recent files

Next release

Starting with the next release, the app remembers the PDFs you opened so it can list them under Recent and reopen them where you left off. It keeps only this:

  • SavedThe file's location on your computer, the page you were on, and your zoom level
  • LimitThe 20 most recent files. Older entries are dropped.
  • Not savedThe document's contents, its text, your searches or your selections
  • WhenWritten when you open or close a file and when you quit the app
  • macOS~/Library/Application Support/org.pdfreader.desktop/recent.tsv
  • Windows%APPDATA%\PDF Reader\recent.tsv

The list never leaves your computer.

Your control

How to delete it

Clear the list: choose File, then Open Recent, then Clear Menu.

Remove everything: quit the app and delete the recent.tsv file shown above. Deleting the app removes the program itself; the recent list is a separate file, so delete it too if you want no trace.

If a file in the list was moved or deleted, choosing it removes it from the list.

The agent panel

You choose who reads along

In development

The agent panel is being built in the open, tracked in issue #18. It is not in any release yet. This is how it is designed to work:

Only the agent you pick. The panel connects to an agent already installed on your computer, such as Claude Code, Codex or Gemini CLI. Parts of your document are shared only with that agent, through that agent's own connection, under its provider's terms.

Reading is not writing. The agent can read the outline, search and read text through the reader's tools. Adding highlights or notes to your document always waits for your approval.

Nothing by default. If you never open the panel, no agent is started and nothing is shared.

This website

No cookies here

This site is a static page. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when the page loads. Downloads are hosted on GitHub Releases, so GitHub's privacy statement applies when you download.

Questions

Something looks wrong?

Agentic PDF is open source, so every claim on this page can be checked in the source code.

To report a privacy concern, open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please leave out personal details and document contents.

This is a plain-language summary of how the app handles data, not a legal policy. When the app's behavior changes, this page changes with it.
