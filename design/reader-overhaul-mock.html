Section 1

Network capacity

Q3 review4 pagesInternal draft
APPROVEDA. Morgan, 4 Nov 2026

Traffic grew steadily through the quarter. The busiest week arrived in late September, when a product launch pushed request volume to capacity limits in two regions for several hours.

Peak traffic

The edge network absorbed the spike without dropped requests. Cache hit rates stayed above target, so origin servers saw only a modest increase in load. Most of the extra traffic was static assets, which the cache serves cheaply.

Two regions ran close to their planned capacity during the launch window. Neither crossed it, but the margin was smaller than the plan assumes for the coming quarter.

Regional load

Load was uneven across regions. The eastern regions carried most of the launch traffic, while the western regions stayed well below their usual peaks. Rebalancing would leave more headroom without adding capacity everywhere. A second launch is not planned this year.

Alex Morgan
Approved, 4 Nov 2026
